About these figures

  • Read from D4H every 2 hours. Training is not included.
  • "Last year" means the same date last year, so the numbers are fair to compare.
  • Team hours = members on the call-out × how long it lasted.
  • A call-out can have more than one root cause and place.
  • No names or casualty details are shown.
  • Figures come from our D4H records and may change as records are updated.
